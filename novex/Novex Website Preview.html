Commercial partnerships, handled properly.

What Novex takes care of, for the creators we represent and for the brands that work with them.

  • Creator representation

    We represent selected creators and act as their commercial partner when sponsorship and partnership opportunities arise.

  • Sponsorship management

    We manage the commercial side of creator partnerships, including communication, negotiation, terms and coordination.

  • Creator selection

    For brand opportunities, we identify creators based on relevance, audience quality, content and genuine fit, not audience size alone.

  • Partnership coordination

    We keep both sides aligned on expectations, deliverables and timelines, so the partnership moves from agreement to execution smoothly.

From first conversation to delivered partnership.

A partnership with Novex moves through four steps, with Novex coordinating between creator and brand throughout.

  1. 1

    Understand

    We learn the creator's content, audience and commercial direction, and what each brand opportunity requires.

  2. 2

    Match

    We assess each opportunity against the creator's content and audience, and move forward only where there is a genuine fit.

  3. 3

    Negotiate

    We handle the commercial conversation: terms, deliverables, timelines and expectations, agreed clearly by both sides.

  4. 4

    Deliver

    We coordinate the agreed partnership and stay involved through execution, as one point of contact for creator and brand.
